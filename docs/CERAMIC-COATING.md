# Ceramic coating: what Mikey uses and why

Saved 2026-11-15 at Mikey's request, after one chat (Nov 7-8) picked CarPro and a
later chat (Nov 15) switched him to Gyeon without knowing why, then switched back.
**Read this before recommending a coating.** Change it only with a new reason, and
write the reason here.

## The pick: CarPro CQuartz UK 3.0

Mikey already uses it (Angelita's car, Sept 2026, half a bottle left). It's the
coating his texts describe ("needs about an hour before it can handle water").

| Surface | Product | Price seen |
|---|---|---|
| Paint, gloss black trim, headlights (2 layers on Pro) | **CarPro CQuartz UK 3.0, 50ml kit with Reload 2.0** | ~$90 (Autocare Genius $89.99, Amazon ~$93, Nov 7) |

Tires: not part of any tier. Normal tire dressing only. Never put paint coating on rubber.

Buy only where the seller is CarPro, Amazon, or an authorized dealer. UK 3.0
expires one year from production and about six months after opening: check the
date, and don't use a bottle that's cloudy, gel-like or crusted at the cap.

## Why UK 3.0 (the reasons that decided it)

His jobs are mobile, often with no garage, and the coating season runs into
Washington's cold, wet months. Those decide it more than the durability number.

| | CarPro UK 3.0 | Gyeon Q2 Syncro EVO |
|---|---|---|
| Working temperature | **40-90 F** (CarPro US page) | about 50 F minimum per a retailer (Carzilla); Gyeon's own figure not found |
| Before it can get wet | **1 hour** (24 best) | 12 hours (Esoteric) |
| Second layer | 45-60 min after the first, **or the next day** | same session |
| Flash | 2-5 min, rainbow shows where it's applied | fast, short wipe window |
| Rated life | **2-5 years** (CarPro US page); CarPro's global page says 18-24 months real world | retailers say ~50 months |

Sources: carpro-us.com/protection/cquartz-uk-3-0-30ml-kit/ ,
carpro.global/product/cq_uk/ , esotericcarcare.com/gyeon-q2-syncro-evo ,
carzilla.ca/products/gyeon-q2-mohs-evo-30ml-50ml (all read 2026-11-15).

Coverage: about 15ml per layer on an average car, about 20ml on a mid-size SUV.
A 50ml bottle does two layers on an SUV with a little left.

## Considered and turned down

- **Gyeon Q2 Syncro EVO** (paint) with Trim EVO and View EVO: longer claimed life,
  but outside its temperature range in a Snohomish County November, 12 hours dry
  before water, and both layers in one session. Fine for a garage job in summer.
- **Gtechniq Crystal Serum Light + EXO v5**: wants an indoor cure (12 hours indoors).
  Was quoted on Aaron's Lightning (Nov 2), never bought.
- **AvalonKing Armor Shield IX**: AvalonKing's own claim is up to 2 years (the IX
  MAX says 3). No published temperature range or time-to-water found, and most
  reviews are the brand's own or affiliate sites. Doesn't back a paid Pro claim.

## The durability claim is still open

The site's Pro tier says 3-5 years, and Mikey texted Kevin "two layers ... hold up
3-5 years". That's the top of CarPro US's 2-5 year range. Angelita was sold UK 3.0
as a "6-12 month coating". Same bottle, three different claims: Mikey should pick
one honest line (the Nov 7 chat suggested "2-3+ years with proper care") and use it
on the site, in quotes and in `mikeysite/PRICING.md`. Ask him; don't change the site
copy without him.

## Job notes that apply every time

- Test the coating on his own car first when the weather is cold, to learn the
  flash time at that temperature.
- Check every panel with a swirl-finder light from two angles before moving on.
  A missed high spot that cures needs a finishing polish and a recoat.
- No car cover on a fresh coating. Pick a dry day; the customer's awning or
  carport is fine. A come-back to fix spotting is included (promised to Kevin).
- A 1-step polish is 6-8 hours (price book). Two weekday afternoons from 1 PM don't
  hold a wash, decon, polish and two coats before dark in Nov-Dec.
